# SWE Essential Skills / Project Planning

Reach a coherent plan for the next commitment, starting with the problem and product rather than a preferred stack. A small tool may need a brief; a multi-party system may need durable decisions and explicit risks. Planning is iterative, not an attempt to settle the entire lifecycle upfront.

## Enter at the actual gap

Use for requested planning, a new product/project, or expansion that changes direction, major boundaries or delivery commitments. A clear request to plan needs no permission to plan again. If a vague idea could mean brainstorming rather than starting a project, clarify that intent before implementing.

Continue adequately planned ongoing work, including substantial features/refactors. A missing document, fresh chat or skill installation is not a new-project event. Recover available decisions and reopen only an affected material gap. Additional discovery, definition, requirements or architecture skills are optional methods for a specific unresolved decision, not prerequisites or mandatory phases. Hand over the established context rather than restarting their workflows. This skill installs no hooks or conversation monitor and does not guarantee host activation.

## Preserve decisions and delegated choices

Product direction, scope, consequential architecture/stack choices and risk acceptance belong to the user or designated owner. Present viable options, practical tradeoffs and a recommendation for unresolved choices. Explicit delegation within stated bounds counts as authority to choose: make the choice, explain consequential tradeoffs and continue within those bounds. It does not authorize new external actions, spending or risk acceptance outside them.

Reuse settled choices; do not ask for them again. A recommendation, draft, apparent convenience, silence or timeout is not consent. An unresolved user-owned choice blocks only dependent work, even if reversible. Continue routine work inside agreed choices and independent authorized exploration; do not infer delegation merely from urgency.

## Recover the evidence

Use the conversation and accessible project evidence first. Read actual contents, not titles/URLs; request only a material missing source or decision. For imported, fragmented, conflicting or inaccessible sources, read [plan intake](references/plan-intake.md). Name unreadable portions and continue independent work without claiming they were reviewed.

Preserve the chosen goal and constraints. Distinguish accepted intent, observed evidence, proposals, and assumptions/unknowns with their consequences. Existing code is evidence of behavior, not necessarily product policy; a polished plan is not evidence of demand or feasibility. Resolve authority conflicts rather than choosing whichever document is newer.

## Resolve what affects the next commitment

Establish enough users/problem, scope, intended outcome, behavior/acceptance and constraints to choose a useful slice. Carry forward an adequate product direction. For missing direction, consequential gaps or cross-lifecycle concerns, use [gap coverage and readiness](references/gap-coverage-and-readiness.md); its coverage lens is not a questionnaire.

- Resolve decisions changing purpose, acceptance, authorization, sensitive data, irreversible effects or major dependencies before dependent commitments.
- Investigate uncertainty that threatens feasibility or substantial rework with a bounded experiment tied to a decision.
- Proceed provisionally within agreed or explicitly delegated bounds, stating limits and revisit triggers; a provisional user-owned choice still needs acceptance.
- Defer later concerns with no current consequence to their latest safe decision point. Do not postpone retention, recovery or deployment constraints that already affect design.

Before asking, check available sources and prior answers; retrieve discoverable facts. Ask only high-impact unresolved choices or unavailable facts. Explain the concrete consequence and recommendation, grouping independent questions while holding dependent ones until prerequisites settle. Use a permitted selectable-input tool for concrete alternatives, or free text for open facts/when unavailable; a clear written answer suffices. Keep permission escalation in its dedicated mechanism.

For an explicit software-plan stress test ("grill me"), software-requirements offers a focused design-interview method when available; carry the existing plan and settled choices into it. Without that skill, investigate consequential counterexamples and ask ready owner decisions at the requested pace.

Each gap can be answered, investigated, handled within agreed/delegated bounds, deferred or dropped as irrelevant. Do not exhaust a questionnaire, repeat answered questions or disguise consequential unowned risks as certainty. A prototype or research proposal is not authorization to contact people, expose artifacts or spend money; do not invent results, thresholds or stakeholder agreement.

## Leave one usable plan and a ready slice

Use the existing canonical artifact and format. Read [usable plan shape](references/usable-plan-shape.md) when a durable plan or consolidation is needed; adapt it instead of filling every heading. Keep decisions, sources, open risks, dependencies and next actions together or linked. Prefer a private authorized draft over an unrequested shared tracker.

Identify a useful end-to-end slice: behavior/acceptance, relevant design/contracts, dependencies, verification and risks resolved. A spike instead has a specific uncertainty, bounded scope, success/failure evidence and a decision afterward; it is not a shipped feature. Adequate conversational decisions can supply this without a new document.

Begin dependent implementation when intent and acceptance avoid consequential guessing, blocking risks are resolved or accepted by the authorized owner, and implementation is requested/authorized. Do not create a redundant approval ritual. A plan-only request ends with the usable plan and next decision; plan approval does not authorize publication, deployment, purchasing or external mutation.

When new evidence contradicts the plan, reopen only affected choices and dependencies. Use the [transition guidance](references/gap-coverage-and-readiness.md#reconcile-lifecycle-transitions-and-changes) for consequential lifecycle changes. Keep accepted/proposed status and superseded reasons clear in the same record; do not silently weaken acceptance or restart discovery.

Report the current direction, ready slice/next action and material decisions or blockers first, linking durable evidence where available. Keep updates direct and specific at meaningful decision/outcome boundaries, respecting the user's cadence; explain scope changes before dependent commitment. State unknowns and unverified claims. A completed plan is not a verified implementation or production-readiness claim.
