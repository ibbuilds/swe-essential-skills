# Product Definition: context, purpose and selection

Product Discovery investigates the opportunity and relevant uncertainty. Product
Definition turns available evidence into choices about target audience, value,
positioning, first useful scope and outcomes. Project Planning orders a buildable
commitment. These are conditional methods around the project's actual gap, not
mandatory sequential phases. An accepted brief can already settle product direction.

The requested correction makes definition inherit conversation, repository,
attachments and authorized linked documents, including their decisions and source
limits. It distinguishes intended launch market and reachable audience from later
expansion. Developer location, language and online availability do not establish
market or jurisdiction. Missing or inaccessible evidence blocks only dependent
commitments; sufficient upstream research is reused. A standalone installation
still works without requiring another skill or a phase-completion receipt.

## Mechanisms and sources

The conditional reference now connects audience/operating setting, actual
alternatives and differentiated value. It considers only conditions that change
adoption or viable scope and separates local, state/regional, national and
international coverage where relevant. An internal tool or developer library may
need organizational or ecosystem reach rather than a geographic launch exercise.
Measurement remains tied to the supported population; a local sample does not
prove global demand. Existing complete-journey, accessibility, operational,
prioritization and owner-authority guidance is retained.

[April Dunford's positioning guide](https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-quickstart-guide-to-positioning)
grounds the relationship between alternatives, distinctive value and best-fit
customers. Her [competition guidance](https://www.aprildunford.com/post/positioning-and-competition)
distinguishes actual customer alternatives from superficially similar products.
The [SBA market-research guide](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/market-research-competitive-analysis)
connects customer location with reach, demand, pricing and competition. Focused
primary-source excerpts were sufficient for these mechanisms; no full-page review
or application of US market data to other jurisdictions is claimed. The skill
contains original instructions rather than copied third-party templates.

## Observed selection and application

One fresh independent executor read all 18 candidate descriptions and chose its
methods before answering six fictional requests. Expected methods and this review
were withheld. It read four selected entrypoints and three relevant references;
the six cases shared that executor context.

| Case | Selected method and observed result |
| --- | --- |
| Rural appointment tool, discovery supplied | Definition proposed reception-focused value and a complete journey within the two accepted municipalities. It retained network, evidence and payment limitations and did not equate statewide ambition with launch coverage. |
| Approved label correction | Construction proposed the narrow edit and contextual check; no new product interview. No edit was claimed without a repository. |
| Defined worldwide offline developer CLI | Planning retained the accepted global audience, free distribution and offline contracts; no local pilot or commercial funnel. |
| Whether another clinic tool merits investment | Discovery identified evidence needed and the lack of authorized research; it did not fabricate findings or define a validated product. |
| Private Notion report returns login page | Definition disclosed unread content, distinguished worldwide ambition from an accepted launch and withheld dependent audience/scope choices. |
| Mexico-to-Spain expansion | Definition preserved accepted Mexican scope, treated Spanish fit as a hypothesis and retained operational/jurisdictional gaps without unauthorized research. |

This checks description-based selection and actual application in a controlled
offline exercise, not live host invocation frequency or all future behavior.
Automatic invocation policy remains enabled. Metadata/local-reference and
skill-creator validation passed separately; those checks do not prove reasoning
quality. No other skill, global instruction, permission, model or integration
configuration changes in this revision.
