# Product Discovery in its real operating context

The individual review starts from `b1c00897153fd17c73e57b22f09c8ff65d21143a`. Product Discovery already covered evidence, actors, alternatives, risk and scoped validation. The main gaps were explicit operating-location grounding and practical local/national/international competitive coverage. The revision adds those mechanisms while reducing generic interaction prose and preserving the accepted-build fast path.

## Scope and ownership

Project Planning already plans the next software commitment; Software Requirements already has the explicit design-interview/grill route. Product Definition owns positioning and scope choices. Those instructions remain unchanged. Product Discovery assesses problem/opportunity uncertainty in the actual project context; no companion installation or duplicate document becomes required.

The new conditional reference covers operating geography, reachable segments, local constraints, geographic competition levels, actual substitutes, source applicability and coverage limits. It separates developer location from service reach and distinguishes a reachable foreign competitor from an unavailable foreign-market analogue. Provider claims may establish their stated terms, not independent demand or quality. Clear internal/implementation tasks retain their existing scope.

Only the requested Product Discovery entrypoint, its UI description, one local reference, scoped evaluation cases and this review change. Automatic discovery remains enabled. No global instructions, permissions, hooks, models, other skills, customers or live product data are changed.

## Verification and limits

Ten new context cases cover operating geography, requested geographic competition levels, international reach, inapplicable statistics, contrary evidence, internal tools, missing material location, incomplete search, research authority and nonvisual substitutes. The original three P01 branches remain unchanged and are reused as contrasts.

One independent expected-answer-blind application pass analyzed six explicitly synthetic local sources and all 13 scenarios. It anchored to the operating market rather than developer location/language, compared local/national/international and manual alternatives, separated available products from analogues, and did not treat an unsupported population claim or enthusiasm as demand. It also distinguished reminder incidents from verified missed appointments rather than inventing a no-show rate. Its recommendation was provisional and left investment to the owner, with no outreach or fabricated research.

Local skill-creator validation and the repository's metadata/reference validator passed. The unchanged repository CI runs its unit suite and validation on Windows and Linux, with a separate aggregate gate. Structural checks do not prove host selection; this application does not establish real-market facts, exhaustive web searches, legal assurance, product success or universal compliance. No external paid API evaluation campaign was run.
